Gaza Genocide

Since October 7, 2023, the Gaza Strip has suffered one of the deadliest assaults in modern history. Continuous bombardments have caused catastrophic human and material losses.

According to humanitarian reports, more than 40,000 Palestinians have been martyred, including over 16,000 children and 10,000 women. Tens of thousands of others have been injured, and thousands remain missing beneath the rubble.

The destruction of infrastructure has been unprecedented: more than 70% of Gaza’s homes and buildings have been damaged or destroyed, along with hospitals, schools, mosques, and vital public facilities. Water, electricity, and medical supplies are almost completely cut off.

Video: Gaza Genocide

International organizations continue to warn of an unfolding humanitarian catastrophe, with displacement, famine, and disease spreading among Gaza’s population of over 2 million people.
