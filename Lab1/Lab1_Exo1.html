BREIF HISTORY

Palestine has been inhabited since ancient times and has seen many civilizations, including the Canaanites, Israelites, Romans, Byzantines, and Ottomans . After World War I, Britain took control under a mandate, and in 1948, the state of Israel was established, leading to the displacement of hundreds of thousands of Palestinians — an event known as the Nakba (“catastrophe”). Since then, the region has faced ongoing conflict over land, refugees, and sovereignty. Today, Palestinians continue to seek independence and international recognition as a sovereign state.
